Privacy policy

Last updated: Mar 25, 2025

GPCRmd is committed to protecting your personal information and complying with applicable data protection laws worldwide, including, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union, the Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Personal Data Protection and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD), and other relevant data protection regulations. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data, and how it applies to your use of GPCRmd's websites, products, and services.


What information does GPCRmd collect?

General user

We voluntarily maintain the following privacy rules:

  • We collect usage statistics with Google Analytics.
  • We take all possible measures to ensure that all detailed usage data remains confidential.
  • Usage data will not be sold or shared with third parties unless for academic purposes, and only after obtaining guarantees that our privacy rules will be followed.
  • Detailed usage data will only be used by site curators for optimization and error detection purposes.
  • We reserve the right to occasionally share usage statistics with granting organizations, ensuring compliance with the privacy rules listed above.

Registered user

We only collect, store, and use your personal data when you register an account with us on our website.This information includes:

  • Your name, username, email and password
  • Your institution & group information for the corresponding acknowledge.

How does GPCRmd use your information?

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Service improvement: To enhance user experience and optimize our services.
  • Customer support: To provide assistance and respond to inquiries.
  • Security & fraud prevention: To protect against unauthorized access and potential threats.

How will GPCRmd share your information?

We do not sell user personal data. However, we may share data with:

  • Legal compliance: If required by law or to protect our rights and interests.
  • International transfers: If applicable, data may be processed outside your country, ensuring compliance with data protection laws.

Cookies

We will automatically collect, store, and use your personal data if you have consented to our use of cookies and similar technologies on our websites, apps, and other software to:

  • Enhance website functionality and performance.
  • Analyze user behavior and improve services.

Please see our Cookie policy for details of how this may apply to you.

Users can manage cookie preferences through their browser settings or opt out via clicking our Cookie policy on the top page link: Update cookies preferences.


User rights

You have the right to request access to, rectification, or erasure of your personal information, as well as the right to restrict or object to its processing. Additionally, you have the right to object to the processing of your personal information based on GPCRmd’s legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@gpcrmd.org.


Data security

We implement strict security measures, including:

  • Encryption of sensitive data.
  • Secure servers and access controls.
  • Regular security audits and monitoring.

Retention of data

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.


Changes to the privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and we may notify users via email or via web.


Contact information

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, you can contact us at: info@gpcrmd.org
