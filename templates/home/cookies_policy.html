Cookies policy

Last updated: March 25, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how GPCRmd ("we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website at https://gpcrmd.org ("Website"). It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

In some cases, cookies may collect personal information or become personal information if combined with other data.


What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your device when you visit a website. They enable website owners to enhance site functionality and collect reporting information.

Cookies set by us are called "first-party cookies," while those set by other parties are called "third-party cookies." Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionalities (e.g., interactive content, analytics).


Why do we use cookies?

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure website functionality ("essential cookies").
  • Enhance performance and functionality.

Cookie Control

You can control cookies via the Cookie Consent Manager (link at the top of the page: Update cookies preferences). Essential cookies cannot be rejected.
